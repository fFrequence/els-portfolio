About

Based in Paris, Edouard Le Scouarnec is a director working across commercial, music video, and narrative formats. Trained in cinematography, he brings a particular attention to light and gesture — finding the precise moment where story and image align.

His work is defined by restraint — clean compositions, deliberate pacing, and a refusal to over-explain. He believes the most powerful images are those that leave room for the viewer. Silence is never emptiness; it is direction.

Currently based in Paris and open to international work. Available for commercial campaigns, branded content, and narrative projects. Fluent in French and English.
